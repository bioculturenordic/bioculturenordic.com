The 2026 Zero-Tolerance Mandate

The regulatory landscape for Danish fish producers is shifting. Are your control measures sufficient?

Urgent Compliance

From July 1, 2026, EU regulations mandate a strict zero-tolerance policy for Listeria monocytogenes in ready-to-eat products throughout their entire shelf life.

The Risk Profile

Cold-smoked salmon and gravad fish remain high-risk categories. Traditional chemical interventions often compromise organoleptic qualities or face consumer rejection.

The Documentation Burden

Producers must provide robust scientific documentation demonstrating that L. monocytogenes cannot grow to harmful levels in their specific matrices.

The Solution

BCN01: Proactive Bioprotection

BCN01 utilizes a selected strain of lactic acid bacteria that naturally outcompetes Listeria monocytogenes without altering the sensory characteristics of your seafood products.

  • ✓ Targeted Action: Specifically active against Listeria spp. at refrigeration temperatures (2°C - 8°C).
  • ✓ Clean Label: Listed simply as "bacterial culture" or "ferment," preserving your clean label status.
  • ✓ Dual Application: Effective for both environmental surface control and direct product application.
